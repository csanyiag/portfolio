City of Pécs

In 2023, our team began developing a brand identity system for the city of Pécs.
The three of us—Eszter Kovács, Dávid Pusztai, and I - worked on the manual under the guidance of Gergely Bőhm, while typographer Oszkár Boskovitz designed the typeface for the city. The concept was developed by Eszter Hudák.
